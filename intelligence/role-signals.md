# Role and capability signals

These are public, generic inferences from the verified site facts. They are not job openings, hiring forecasts, personal fit claims, or application recommendations.

## Status definitions

- **VERIFIED OPEN ROLE:** an active official SpaceX posting with an exact URL, title, location, and check date.
- **RECENTLY OBSERVED ROLE:** a previously observed official posting that is no longer confirmed open; it cannot support a current-vacancy claim.
- **INFERRED ROLE FAMILY:** a capability family inferred from project scope; it does not claim a vacancy exists.
